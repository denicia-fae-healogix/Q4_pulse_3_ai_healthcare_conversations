Eat regularly enough to support training, and aim for 7–9 hours of sleep when possible.  Aim for consistency, not perfection.  You can still enjoy Caribbean dishes, restaurant meals, desserts and snacks. Focus on what you do most of the time instead of labeling foods as good or bad. Try this flexible routine instead:  * Pick three proteins: for example, chicken, eggs and beans, with Greek yogurt for snacks. * Prepare two carbohydrates: rice and roasted potatoes, or rice and pasta. * Choose three vegetables and two fruits: include frozen options to make your food last longer. * Cook two main dishes: one for the first part of the week and one for later in the week. * Keep backup meals available: eggs on toast, tuna wraps, yogurt bowls or beans and rice for nights when you're too busy to cook.
